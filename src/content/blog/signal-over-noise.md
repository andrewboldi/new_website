---

It's so imperative to discern the signal over the noise. You need to continually ask
yourself, *"what is the most important thing"* — for a particular system, for whatever
you're doing, for anything at all. You need to cut away the noise and focus on the most
significant parts.

For example, asking *"what are the limitations of this machine learning model"* is such
a great way to gauge the places where we can actually improve. It immediately collapses a
fog of possibilities down to the few that matter.

It's great, of course, to be excited by something — to come up with new ideas and to
become a visionary. But you need to remain close to the truth to make real advancements.
Excitement points you at a direction; signal tells you whether the direction is real.
